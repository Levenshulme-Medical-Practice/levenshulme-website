<h2 id="non-nhs-services">Non-NHS services</h2>

Some services are not covered by the NHS, such as medicals for sport, work or HGV and PSV licences, and insurance forms. These are done by appointment and we charge a fee. Completed forms are usually ready to collect from reception after 48 hours.
