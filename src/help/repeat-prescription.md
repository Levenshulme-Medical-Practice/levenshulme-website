The quickest way to order medicines on your repeat list is the [NHS App](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/). You can also hand in or post your repeat slip.

We do not take prescription requests over the phone, and we do not accept requests from pharmacies. Please allow **two working days** for us to process your request, not counting weekends and bank holidays.

If the medicine you need is not on your repeat list, or you have a question about a medicine, [ask us a question about your medication](/help/medication-question/).
