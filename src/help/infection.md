Through the NHS Pharmacy First service, pharmacists can assess and treat these conditions without you seeing a GP, and can give prescription medicine, including antibiotics, if you need it:

- sinusitis (age 12 and over)
- sore throat (age 5 and over)
- earache (age 1 to 17)
- infected insect bites (age 1 and over)
- impetigo (age 1 and over)
- shingles (age 18 and over)
- urinary tract infections in women aged 16 to 64

You don’t need an appointment. If the pharmacist thinks you need to see a GP, they will tell you.

Many infections are caused by viruses and get better on their own. Antibiotics don’t work for these, and taking them when they’re not needed can cause side effects and makes them less effective in future.
