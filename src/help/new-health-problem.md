Our online request is for problems that are **not urgent**. A member of our clinical team will read it and decide what help you need. This might be an appointment, a phone call, advice, or a suggestion to see another service such as a pharmacist.

We respond to urgent requests the same day and to other requests by the end of the next working day. You can also call us on [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033) during our [opening hours](/opening-hours/).

Some problems can be dealt with quicker elsewhere:

- [I think I have an infection](/help/infection/): a pharmacist can treat many common infections.
- [I have a muscle or joint problem](/help/muscle-joint/): you can refer yourself to a physiotherapist.
