You can send us a question about your medicines online at any time during our opening hours. Our pharmacy team or a clinician will reply.

You can also call us on [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033). Calls about medication are best after 12.30pm.

For general advice about a medicine, a [local pharmacist](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/pharmacy/find-a-pharmacy) can help without an appointment.
