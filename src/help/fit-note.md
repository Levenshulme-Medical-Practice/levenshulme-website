You do not need a fit note for the first **7 days** you are off sick. Your employer may ask you to fill in a self-certification form instead.

If you have been off for longer than 7 days, or need your fit note extended, send us a request online. Tell us the dates you need covering and why you are unable to work. We may contact you to discuss it.

If your health problem is new or getting worse, please also [tell us about it](/help/new-health-problem/) so we can make sure you get the right care.
