description: How to get medical help when Levenshulme Medical Practice is closed.
reviewed: 2026-10-31
---
- **Emergency:** call 999 or go to A&E.
- **Urgent but not an emergency:** use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call 111. They can arrange for you to see a GP or other clinician if you need to.
- **Minor illness:** a [local pharmacist](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/pharmacy/find-a-pharmacy) can help without an appointment, and many are open late and at weekends.
- **Not urgent:** contact us when we next open. You can [send us an online request](/help/) during our opening hours.

Evening and Saturday appointments are also available through our Primary Care Network. See [appointments](/appointments/) for details.
