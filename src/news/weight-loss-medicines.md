Weight loss drugs (Ozempic, Mounjaro) are not currently available on NHS prescription in Greater Manchester. For more information and to stay up to date with their availability, see [weight management in Greater Manchester](https://gmintegratedcare.org.uk/weight-management-in-greater-manchester/).
