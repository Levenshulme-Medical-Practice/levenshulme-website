When you use the right NHS service, you get the right treatment sooner.

- **Pharmacy:** advice and treatment for minor illnesses, and some infections through [Pharmacy First](/help/infection/).
- **Your GP practice:** for health problems that won’t go away or need a clinician. [See everything we can help with](/help/).
- **NHS 111:** urgent help when you’re not sure where to go, day or night. [Use NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call 111.
- **999 or A&E:** for life-threatening emergencies only.
