When you have a test, we will tell you roughly how long the result should take. Once a clinician has reviewed it, most results appear in the [NHS App](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/).

If we need to discuss a result with you, we will contact you or invite you for an appointment. To check a result, please call [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033) after 2.00pm.

We will only give results to the person they belong to, unless that person has given permission or is not able to understand them. Our reception team cannot interpret results, but can read you any comment a clinician has added.

Laboratories sometimes flag a result as slightly outside the normal range. Our clinicians look at this alongside everything else they know about you, and a small change is often nothing to worry about.

## How long results usually take

| Test | Usual wait |
|---|---|
| Blood tests | 2 days to 1 week |
| Urine tests | 4 to 7 days |
| Stool (faecal) tests | 4 to 7 days |
| Swabs | 1 week |
| Ultrasound report | 1 to 2 weeks |
| MRI scan report | 1 to 2 weeks |
| X-ray report | 2 to 3 weeks |
| Cervical screening | 2 to 3 weeks |

## Blood tests

Our nurses and healthcare assistants only take blood when one of our clinicians has asked for it. [Find out more about blood tests on the NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/blood-tests/).
