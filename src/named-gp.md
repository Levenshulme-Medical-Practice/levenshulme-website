Every patient has a named GP who is responsible for their overall care at the practice. You will be told who your named GP is when you register.

Having a named GP does not mean you can only see that doctor. You can still be seen by any of our clinicians, and you may see someone else if that means you get help sooner.

If you would like to know who your named GP is, or would prefer a different named GP, please ask at reception.
