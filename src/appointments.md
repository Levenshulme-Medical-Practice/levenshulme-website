You need an appointment to see any of our clinical team. You don’t need to contact us at 8am: get in touch at any time during our [opening hours](/opening-hours/) and we will assess your request and respond, the same day if it’s urgent and by the end of the next working day if it’s not.

## How to ask for an appointment

- **Online.** [Send us a request](/help/new-health-problem/) during our opening hours, or use the [NHS App](https://www.nhsapp.service.nhs.uk/login).
- **Phone.** Call [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033).
- **In person.** Visit reception at Dunstable Street.

You can book an appointment up to four weeks in advance. If your problem only needs advice, we may offer you a phone call instead of a face-to-face appointment.

We only carry out blood tests if one of our clinicians has asked for them.

## Who will I see?

Not every problem needs a doctor. Depending on what you need, you may see a GP, practice nurse, pharmacist, healthcare assistant or another member of our team. If you want to see a specific doctor, you may have to wait longer.

## Urgent appointments

If your problem is urgent, we aim to help you the same day. Same-day appointments are given based on clinical need, so you may not see the doctor of your choice.

Sick notes, repeat prescriptions and referrals are not suitable for urgent appointments.

Our reception team will ask for some details about your problem so that our clinicians can decide how urgently you need to be seen. Everything you tell them is kept confidential.

## Home visits

Please come to the surgery if you can, as this is where we have our equipment. If you are housebound or too ill to come in, call us before 10.00am. A GP will decide whether a home visit is needed and how urgently.

## Early, evening and weekend appointments

We offer some appointments from 7.00am to 8.00am. Through Gorton and Levenshulme Primary Care Network, appointments are also available on weekday evenings from 6.30pm to 8.00pm and on Saturday mornings at West Gorton Medical Centre, West Point Medical Centre or Hawthorn Medical Centre. You may see a doctor you haven’t met before, but they will be able to see your medical record. Ask our reception team about these appointments. They cannot be booked online.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling an appointment</h2>

If you can’t come to your appointment, please let us know as soon as possible so we can offer it to someone else. Cancel in the [NHS App](https://www.nhsapp.service.nhs.uk/login), call [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033) or tell reception.
