If you take a medicine for a long time, we can add it to your repeat list so you can order it without seeing us each time. We review your repeat medicines at least once a year.

## How to order

- **Online** in the [NHS App](https://www.nhsapp.service.nhs.uk/login).
- **In person or by post.** Hand in or post your repeat slip to the surgery.

We do not take prescription requests over the phone, to help prevent mistakes, and we do not accept requests from pharmacies. Only items on your repeat list can be ordered. Please allow **two working days**, not counting weekends and bank holidays.

## Nominating a pharmacy

You can choose a pharmacy for us to send your prescriptions to electronically, so you don’t need to collect a paper prescription. Choose or change your pharmacy in the NHS App, by calling [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033), or at reception.

## Questions about your medication

- **Online.** [Send us a medication question](/help/medication-question/) at any time during our opening hours.
- **Phone.** Call [0161 225 4033](tel:+441612254033). Calls about medication are best after 12.30pm.

## Medication reviews

We need to review your repeat medicines at least once a year. We will tell you when your review is due. Please book it promptly to avoid delays to your prescriptions.

## Local pharmacies

Pharmacists are experts in medicines and can help with many minor health problems, including some infections through [Pharmacy First](/help/infection/). Many are open late and at weekends, and you don’t need an appointment. [Find a pharmacy near you](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/pharmacy/find-a-pharmacy).
